FAQ

Everything you need to know before you start.

Do I need a login?

No. This version is built as a front-end experience without authentication, so you can use it immediately in the browser.

Can I generate final PDFs?

Yes. Once your documents are matched and requirements are valid, you can export the final tender package as a PDF.

Does it work with requirements.json?

Yes. The app expects a structured JSON tender file and validates it before matching documents.

Can it be expanded later?

Absolutely. The current design is ready for backend storage, team workspaces, and payment-based product features.
